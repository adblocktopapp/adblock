Information We Collect

AdFilter All does not require account registration and does not ask users to create an account.

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, contacts, photos, videos, messages, health data, financial information, or precise location data.

Advertising data collected through third-party advertising services is not used by us to identify you personally.

These identifiers may be used for third-party advertising and analytics purposes. We do not link this data to your identity.

Usage Data

Usage Data may include information about how the Application is used, such as app launches, taps, interactions with features, ad impressions, ad interactions, and similar app activity.

Usage Data may also include device information such as device type, operating system, app version, approximate technical information, performance data, and diagnostic data.

Diagnostics Data

We or our third-party service providers may collect crash data and performance data to improve app stability, fix bugs, monitor performance, and maintain app functionality.

Diagnostics data is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking purposes.

The Company may use collected information for the following purposes:

  • App Functionality: to provide, maintain, secure, and improve the Application.

  • Analytics: to understand how users interact with the Application and improve features and performance.

  • Advertising: to display ads, measure ad performance, and prevent advertising fraud.

  • Diagnostics: to detect crashes, improve stability, and resolve technical issues.

  • Purchases: to process and restore in-app purchases or subscriptions through Apple App Store or Google Play billing systems. Payment information is handled by Apple or Google, and we do not receive your payment card details.

Data Linked to Your Identity

We do not link collected device identifiers, usage data, advertising data, crash data, or performance data to your personal identity.
