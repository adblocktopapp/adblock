Privacy Policy for AdFilter All

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AdFilter All ("AdFilter All", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the AdFilter All mobile application ("Application").

We are committed to protecting your privacy and to being transparent about the information processed by the Application and by third-party services used by the Application.

1. Information We Do Not Require

AdFilter All does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, phone number, home address, contacts, photos, videos, messages, health information, or precise location information in order to use the Application.

We do not request or intentionally collect payment card numbers or other payment credentials. Payments for purchases and subscriptions are processed by the applicable App Store or Google Play billing system.

2. Information Processed by AdFilter All

2.1 Local Application Data

AdFilter All stores certain application settings and usage-related information locally on your device. This may include settings required to operate the Application, information about whether certain features are enabled, timestamps, and locally calculated usage statistics.

Examples may include information used to remember application preferences, advertising display timing, feature status, and statistics such as estimated time or data saved by the Application.

This locally stored information is used to operate and personalize the Application's functionality and is not intentionally transmitted by AdFilter All to our own servers.

2.2 DNS Protection

When you enable the DNS protection feature, AdFilter All configures the device to use DNS-over-HTTPS provided by AdGuard DNS.

The DNS service configured by AdFilter All uses the following public AdGuard DNS endpoints:

  • 94.140.14.14
  • 94.140.15.15

When DNS protection is enabled, AdFilter All configures the device to use AdGuard DNS over HTTPS. DNS requests are processed through the AdGuard DNS service configured by the Application.

AdFilter All does not operate the DNS servers used by this feature and does not receive, store, or maintain DNS query logs on its own servers.

When DNS protection is enabled, DNS requests may therefore be processed by AdGuard DNS. AdGuard's handling of DNS requests and related information is governed by AdGuard's own privacy policy: AdGuard DNS Privacy Policy .

AdGuard states that its public DNS service processes information necessary to provide DNS functionality and may retain aggregated or anonymized information for service operation, performance, and security purposes. AdGuard's privacy practices may change, and users should review the current AdGuard DNS Privacy Policy.

2.3 Advertising Information

AdFilter All uses Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements.

The Application requests non-personalized advertisements. Non-personalized advertising is not based on the user's previous browsing or application activity for the purpose of personalized ad targeting.

However, non-personalized advertisements may still involve technical information, device identifiers, cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as frequency capping, aggregated reporting, fraud prevention, and technical ad delivery.

Depending on the version of the Google Mobile Ads SDK and applicable configuration, Google may process information such as IP address, device identifiers, advertising information, crash information, performance information, and information about interactions with advertisements or the Application.

Google describes its current Mobile Ads SDK data practices here: Google Mobile Ads SDK Data Disclosure .

Google also provides information about its advertising serving modes here: Google Ad Serving Modes .

2.4 Advertising Identifiers and Device Information

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process device-level information and identifiers that are available to the SDK, subject to the device operating system, applicable permissions, SDK configuration, and Google's policies.

AdFilter All does not intentionally use advertising identifiers to create a personal profile of you.

Third-party advertising providers may process information independently according to their own privacy policies.

2.5 Diagnostics and Performance Information

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process technical diagnostic and performance information, including information related to crashes, application performance, and advertising performance.

AdFilter All does not operate a separate analytics or crash-reporting service such as Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Sentry, Amplitude, or Mixpanel.

Information processed by third-party SDKs is governed by the respective third party's privacy policies.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by AdFilter All or its third-party service providers may be used for the following purposes:

  • Application functionality: to provide and maintain the features of AdFilter All.
  • Application settings: to remember settings and feature states on your device.
  • DNS protection: to configure and disable the DNS protection feature at your request.
  • Advertising: to display non-personalized advertisements and support advertising delivery, measurement, frequency capping, and fraud prevention.
  • Diagnostics and performance: to identify technical problems and improve Application stability and performance, where such information is processed by applicable third-party SDKs.
  • Purchases: to provide access to purchased features and restore eligible purchases or subscriptions.

4. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

AdFilter All may offer in-app purchases and auto-renewable subscriptions through Apple's App Store and, where applicable, Google Play.

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google using their respective billing systems. AdFilter All does not receive or store your payment card number, bank account number, or payment credentials.

The Application may receive information necessary to determine whether a purchase or subscription is active and to provide the corresponding features.

Apple and Google may process transaction information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Sharing

AdFilter All does not sell your personal information.

AdFilter All does not operate a personal data advertising database and does not sell user profiles to advertisers.

Information may be processed by third-party providers when necessary to provide Application functionality, advertising, DNS functionality, or payment services.

The principal third-party services relevant to the Application include:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) — advertising and related technical services.
  • AdGuard DNS — DNS-over-HTTPS resolution and DNS filtering when DNS protection is enabled.
  • Apple App Store — application distribution and in-app purchases on Apple devices.
  • Google Play — application distribution and in-app purchases on supported Android devices.

6. Tracking

AdFilter All does not intentionally use information collected by the Application to track you across third-party applications or websites for personalized advertising.

The Application requests non-personalized advertising from Google Mobile Ads. Nevertheless, third-party advertising services may process technical information according to their own policies and applicable requirements.

Non-personalized advertising does not mean that no technical identifiers or network information can be processed. Google states that non-personalized ads may still use cookies or mobile ad identifiers for frequency capping and aggregated ad reporting.

7. Third-Party Services

AdFilter All uses third-party services to provide certain functionality. These services may process information independently from AdFilter All.

Google's privacy information is available at: Google Privacy Policy .

Google's advertising information is available at: Google Advertising Technologies .

AdGuard DNS

When DNS protection is enabled, AdFilter All uses AdGuard DNS as its DNS-over-HTTPS provider.

8. Data Retention

AdFilter All does not maintain a database of user accounts or a server-side database containing the Application's locally stored usage statistics.

Information stored locally by the Application remains on your device until it is removed by the Application, overwritten, or the Application is deleted, subject to the operating system's behavior.

Third-party providers may retain information according to their own retention policies. Please review the applicable third-party privacy policies for details.

9. Your Choices

You can control the DNS protection feature through the Application.

You can disable DNS protection through the Application at any time.

You can also remove locally stored Application data by deleting the Application from your device, subject to the behavior of the operating system and third-party services.

Advertising and privacy controls provided by Apple, Google, or other third-party services may also be available depending on your device, region, and applicable settings.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding personal information processed by third-party service providers, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing.

Because certain information is processed directly by third-party services such as Google or AdGuard, requests concerning information held by those providers may need to be submitted directly to the relevant provider.

You may contact us using the information provided below if you have questions about information processed by AdFilter All.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the information handled by AdFilter All.

However, no method of electronic transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Children's Privacy

AdFilter All is not directed specifically to children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly request or collect personal information directly from children under 13 through an account registration process because AdFilter All does not require account registration.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

13. International Data Processing

Third-party service providers used by AdFilter All may process information on servers located in countries other than your country of residence.

Such processing is subject to the privacy policies and safeguards of the applicable third-party service providers and applicable law.

14. Links to Third-Party Websites

The Application may contain links to websites or services operated by third parties.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites or services.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of each third-party service before using it.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Application, third-party services, applicable law, or our privacy practices.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
